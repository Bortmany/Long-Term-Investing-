# InvestIQ AI — Competitor Research (2026-09-01)

**Purpose:** feature inspiration and a view of what a paid version of InvestIQ AI could look like. This is **not** a launch decision — signups stay closed, no payments are being added as a result of this report.

## 0. What's actually built (verified against the repo, not just the docs)

`README.md` and the top of `docs/BUILD-PLAN.md` describe the project as "Phase 1 done, Phase 2 half done, Phases 3–6 designed but not started." **That description is stale.** The `STATUS` section further down `docs/BUILD-PLAN.md`, and the actual routes on disk (`src/app/(app)/{portfolio,stocks,theses,committee,reviews,watchlist,settings}`), show all 8 phases (through an owner-requested Phase 7 and a Phase 8 launch-readiness pass) built, verified and code-reviewed, plus six rounds of post-hoc security fixes in the git history (rate-limit DoS, oversell guard, atomic writes, shared-price poisoning, one-portfolio-per-user, anonymous rate-limit bucketing). This is a materially more complete product than its own README currently advertises — worth a doc fix separately, but noted here so the gap analysis below is judged against the real thing.

Built today: sign-in, single portfolio per user, transactions (manual + CSV import wizard), FX rates, holdings/cash/dividend math, dashboard (returns, allocation donuts, dividend module), stock pages (profile, price chart, statements, ratios, dividends), AI Portfolio Health Score, Thesis Tracker (create/check/close, integrity sparkline), AI Investment Committee (6 personas + synthesis, buy/sell analysis), Weekly Review + news summaries, price/drop/thesis-due alerts, ~30-term glossary, privacy/terms/data-export/account-deletion. All AI surfaces are dormant ("connect your key") without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; US live prices need `FMP_API_KEY`; everything else uses honestly-badged manual/sample data.

## 3. User sentiment (Trustpilot, app-store and review-site mining; Reddit note below)

- **Simply Wall St** — Trustpilot 4.5/5 across ~4,800 reviews, liked for its visual "Snowflake" presentation. Recurring complaint: brokerage sync doesn't stay current and "requires manual intervention," so balances drift out of accurate — exactly the silent-staleness problem InvestIQ's source-badge/`derived`/`manual-as-of-date` model is built to make impossible to hide. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/simplywall.st)
- **getquin** — Trustpilot ~3.8–4.0/5. Praised for interface and portfolio visualization; recurring complaints are data-accuracy issues and difficulty integrating some brokerage accounts. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/getquin.com)
- **Seeking Alpha Quant Ratings** — the company cites a University of Kentucky backtest claiming strong predictive power; independent user commentary found was more skeptical, one review summarizing the ratings as **"questionable at best."** This is the gimmicky-AI-score pattern InvestIQ's evidence+reasoning+confidence+disclaimer AI panels are explicitly designed not to repeat. [Quant Ratings FAQ](https://help.seekingalpha.com/premium/quant-ratings-and-factor-grades-faq)
- **Stock Rover** — largely positive among users who push past the learning curve; two repeated gripes: no dedicated mobile app, and price is hard to justify on a small portfolio. [Review roundup](https://www.wallstreetzen.com/blog/stock-rover-review/)
- **Sharesight** — mixed; one visible complaint cited missing "gain/loss in dollar terms" after switching from a free tool — the "the tool shows something but not the number I actually need" complaint pattern that always-computed, source-badged figures avoid. [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/sharesight/reviews)

**Evidence-quality note:** direct `site:reddit.com` queries (r/investing, r/dividends, r/Bogleheads) largely failed to surface actual Reddit threads — search kept returning Trustpilot/App Store/aggregator pages instead. The sentiment above is real and sourced, but it's review-site sentiment, not specifically-Reddit sentiment. **[Guessing]** that Reddit sentiment tracks these same findings (staleness/accuracy complaints, skepticism of AI scores) — flagged as a research gap rather than presented as confirmed Reddit consensus.

## 5. If this ever went commercial — positioning note

InvestIQ AI's most defensible angle is **not** "another AI stock scorer" — that category (Simply Wall St, Seeking Alpha Quant Ratings) is already crowded and is the exact thing user sentiment is skeptical of. The defensible angle is the combination of (a) a market nobody else in this research covers — GCC investors tracking MSX/Tadawul/DFM alongside US holdings — and (b) a data-honesty guarantee that is structural (typed "unavailable," source badges, no fabricated fallback) rather than a marketing claim, sitting directly on top of the sync/accuracy complaints that recur against the incumbents. A plausible price band, based on the competitor set above, is the getquin/Snowball/Ziggma tier (~$80–150/yr) rather than Sharesight's cheaper tax-report tier or Seeking Alpha's content-volume tier — InvestIQ isn't selling tax reports or article volume, it's selling trustworthy portfolio truth plus auditable AI analysis. Inspiration only — the repo stays single-owner/private with signups closed; nothing here implies a launch decision.

Reddit-specific searches mostly failed to surface actual threads; sentiment here is review-site-sourced, flagged as a gap.

- **Advantages:** the golden rule (source badges, no fabricated fallback) directly answers the top competitor complaint; AI panels with evidence+reasoning+disclaimer+spend-cap directly avoid the black-box-score distrust pattern; Thesis Tracker + multi-persona AI Committee has no equivalent in the surveyed set.
