## What InvestIQ gives

**Free:**
- One portfolio.
- Transactions, CSV import and broker file presets.
- Dashboard, stock pages, glossary and source badges.
- Gulf and US holdings.
- Basic price alerts.
- Data export and account deletion. These are data rights, so they are never behind Pro. (Changed by the session from the researcher's draft, which listed export under Pro.)
- 2 new AI analyses a day.

**Pro:**
- Everything in Free.
- 10 new AI analyses a day.
- Full Investment Committee.
- Thesis check-ups and weekly AI review.
- "Time to review" alerts.
- Broker connection and Sharia badge.

Core tracking stays free, so people trust the app before paying.

## Price

**Pro: $9.99 a month, or $89 a year** (about 26% off, roughly three months free). This sits at the low end of the Snowball and Sharesight range. It gives Gulf users something the others do not offer.

## AI limits and your Anthropic bill

A Committee run is about 7 model calls. The estimate is $0.15 to $0.25, so we use **$0.20**. A Health Score run is about $0.03. This assumes mid-price Sonnet-class models and is not measured, so check it against the real Anthropic bill.

- **Free, 2 a day:** worst case is 2 x 30 days x $0.20 = **$12 a month** per heavy user. Most free users will cost under $1. Runs that reuse an existing result cost nothing.
- **Pro, 10 a day:** worst case is $60 a month, which is more than the price. Real use is far lower, likely 5 to 10 runs a month, or about $1 to $2. Add a monthly ceiling per Pro user (for example 150 runs) so no one can cost more than they pay.
- Also set one overall daily spend limit for the whole app, so a wave of sign-ups cannot run up a large bill.

## Gulf angle

None of the five shows Muscat (MSX), Saudi (Tadawul) or Dubai (DFM) support. Gulf investors have no good tracker, so they will pay for one that works. Most Gulf countries have no personal capital-gains or dividend tax, which removes Sharesight's main selling point. Reasonable prices matter here. Card payment in USD suits the Gulf, where the Omani rial, Saudi riyal and UAE dirham are all fixed to the dollar or close to it.

Unconfirmed: Stock Rover's own page and third-party sites disagree on prices; Simply Wall St's Unlimited monthly price.

## Recommendation: Launch Free (2 analyses a day) and Pro at $9.99 a month or $89 a year (10 analyses a day), with a per-user monthly ceiling and a whole-app daily spend limit.
